# When each ruin became known

A ruin that was never lost is known to the viewpoints living near it from the day it fell. Every other viewpoint
knows it from its own recorded encounter (`found`), or from a year it became known the world over (`widely`);
failing both, from 1900, when the cultural viewpoints share one map. A culture's encounter reaches its knowledge
community while it belongs (Britain's reaches Europe). These dates are approximate; *draft* rows still want a citation.
