# Found-again landmark sketchbook

One archaeological study for each of the 69 cities in the **Found again** collection. These use the existing `city` depiction format and the normal landmark navigation, image sizes, lightbox and Chinese translation path.

## Scope and time

Each `rediscovered` era begins at the city’s existing rediscovery date. The image represents its archaeological phase, including remains exposed by subsequent research; it is **not** a reconstruction of the first day of discovery. It becomes available from that phase onward. Existing city dates and their uncertainty are retained. Local knowledge is acknowledged where relevant; rediscovery does not imply that everyone had forgotten the place.

Scenes alternate streets, architectural interiors, landscape settings, earthworks and close studies of finds. Archaeological remains stay ruined rather than being rebuilt into speculative ancient cities. Pattanam is presented as a candidate for Muziris. The location of Sarai is described through associated archaeological sites.

## Captions and sources

Captions use two natural narrative sentences: what is visible, followed by what it reveals about the place. English is stored in each `subject.json`; Chinese is in `data/i18n/zh/depictions.json`. Sources are registered as `found-study-<city>` and linked in each scene’s evidence and generation record.

Artwork was generated with the built-in imagegen tool. The exact prompt and reference URLs are saved beside each `rediscovered.webp` as `rediscovered.json`. The normal build makes thumbnail, card and full-size variants.
